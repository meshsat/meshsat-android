A field kit on the LAN has its own card under Setup, with its API token. A public warning a kit relays sounds the alarm and stays under Setup, Safety, Warnings. A voice note or a photo rides in a Reticulum message. Nothing goes online before you ask for it: the map loads tiles once you open it, orbit data is downloaded once a satellite modem is set up, the welcome asks for each permission once, and SMS log lines carry no number or text.
